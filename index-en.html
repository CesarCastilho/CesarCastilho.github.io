Academic profile

About

I am César Castilho, Associate Professor at the Department of Mathematics of the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), Brazil.

My academic work involves research and teaching in applied mathematics, with particular interests in mathematical epidemiology, dynamical systems, population dynamics, and mathematical modeling of biological and ecological systems.

I am also involved in undergraduate and graduate education, particularly in differential equations, mathematical modeling, and computational methods.

Research areas

Research

My current research focuses primarily on mathematical models for infectious disease dynamics, arbovirus persistence, spatial heterogeneity, and nonlinear dynamical systems.

Mathematical Epidemiology

Mathematical modeling of transmission, persistence, and dynamics of infectious diseases.

Dynamical Systems

Qualitative analysis of nonlinear models and applications to biological systems.

Mathematical Modeling

Deterministic, stochastic, and computational models applied to complex systems.
